Autonomous Candidate Protection

Smart Application Safeguards

Automated ATS screeners and recruiters disqualify thousands of qualified applicants due to subtle framing misalignments. JACX's built-in defense engine actively guards your applications before you submit.

🛡️
Seniority Defense

Role-Level Calibrator (Overqualified Protector)

Applying for an Individual Contributor (IC) or mid-level role? This safeguard automatically downweights executive and director-level framing from your profile search chunks, preventing automatic "overqualification" rejections by recruiters.

safeguards.overqualifiedProtector = true
🎯
Specialist Focus

Focused Skill Pruning (Specialist over Generalist)

Listing 40 technologies makes you look unfocused. This policy automatically prioritizes skills and experiences directly relevant to the specific Job Description (JD), pruning irrelevant legacy tools and highlighting laser-focused competency.

safeguards.skillPruning = true
🎭
Voice Tuning

Culture & Tone Alignment (Startup vs. Enterprise)

Automatically infers employer archetypes from job postings. Injects high-velocity ownership language for early-stage startups (“shipped rapid MVPs, full-lifecycle ownership”) or governance and compliance emphasis for enterprise organizations.

safeguards.toneAlignment = true
💵
Comp Safety

Salary Band Guard (Auto-Anchoring)

Prevents pricing yourself out of consideration or undervaluing your worth. Intelligently anchors suggested compensation proposals strictly to the employer's published salary band or your target threshold.

safeguards.salaryBandGuard = true
🔍
Anti-Hallucination

Evidence Grounding Rule

Strictly prohibits unverified buzzwords, hyperbolic claims, or fabricated metrics. Every suggestion or cover letter sentence requires direct citation to local candidate profile facts and verified work achievements.

safeguards.evidenceGrounding = true
⚙️
Full Control

Individual Policy Switches & Master Toggle

You remain in total command. Toggle individual safeguards on or off based on your preferences, or disable the entire suite with one click from the Settings view. Dual-persisted across IndexedDB and local storage.

smart_safeguards_enabled = true
